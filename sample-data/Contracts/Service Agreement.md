# Service Agreement

Project: Project Apollo
Service provider: Contoso Managed Services

## Support window

Contoso provides weekday support from 08:00 to 18:00 CET. Weekend support is
available only with ten business days of advance notice.

## Cutover support

The current Project Apollo cutover window is planned for a weekend. If the
cutover date moves, Contoso requires the project team to confirm the revised
weekend support booking at least ten business days before the new date.

## Risk note

If the supplier hardware delay forces a late cutover change, weekend support may
not be available for the revised date.
